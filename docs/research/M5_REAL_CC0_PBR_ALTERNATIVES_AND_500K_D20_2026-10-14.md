## Genuine 500K mapping A/B via Blender (technical proof)

`tools/blender/apply_licensed_cc0_black_stone_d20.py` imports the unchanged, **real single D20 indexed-500,000-triangle** engraved master GLB SHA256 `67650874fe979f6b7b82ad0e596fd5ea8d3397c58c50fa0d5cf8e4c83c9edadf`, retains **20 engraved gold mesh objects and matching PBR gold**. The original 5×4 source face-UV tiles are re-mapped to one material repeat per triangle face. Edges separately remain satin; stone faces use authentic CC0 normal and roughness with physically appropriate dielectric IOR 1.49, metallic=0, clearcoat highlights. GLB has actual source PBR channels, not just colored shader values.

- Variant `as_downloaded`: all three original 1024 source color/normal/roughness maps untouched. Actual Blender Cycles evidence shows **unsuitable striking white marble veins** across facets, so reject as final look.
- Variant `subdued_black`: **declared derivative, not an untouched external texture**. Source spatial diffuse data is dark-mapped in neutral black instead of white veins; original CC0 normal preserved, original roughness minimally calibrated to approx 0.08–0.19; still no purple. Actual Blender Cycles reference shows black faceted die but glossy reflections currently **too weak / underlit** for owner's desired polished obsidian. This remains a look-development candidate, NOT owner-approved.
