# Dice rules

Supported final pool: D4 D6 D8 D10 D12 D20 D100, D100 either single 100-face physical die or two distinct D10s (tens and ones, where 00 + 0 = 100). Distinguish natural face, selected natural face after D20 advantage/disadvantage, applied modifiers and final sum. Critical only on natural 1/20 of the selected D20, regardless of modifier.

Default single-type quick roll. Advanced mode: mixed groups, formulas, quantities, reroll specific values, modifiers, separately selected keep/drop advantage/disadvantage. Preserve full roll trace, formula, result and timing. Rejection sampling maps secure bytes to each discrete side uniformly. Test-only injected entropy is never used for production rolls.
