# Design / direction

**Approved:** minimalist variant 3 from Discovery, *replacing its tilted scene with perfectly overhead fixed camera*.

- Main surface occupies majority of portrait viewport. Controls are a compact bottom dock; advanced mechanics behind a secondary panel.
- No immersive backdrop initially. Table material black velvet, dice black obsidian with legible contrasted engraved numerals and physical geometry, illuminated without glare.
- Throw from unseen hand-like direction at top; no hand animation, static camera, no artificial camera shakes or tilts.
- Authentic collision audio and haptics proportional to contact, not a looped cosmetic sound.
- Natural 1 / 20 on D20: origin-bound critical failures/success aura + flash; other dice never trigger critical effect.
- Top-left drawer: real configuration choices only. Cosmetics catalog reserved for later stages; no visible dead ends.
- During throw, lock pan/scroll/dock control to prevent accidental interactions. After settlement, show copyable breakdown separate from die face.

Source photographs and exploratory generated mockups are direction references, not licensed textures or final approved assets. No copying RPGD100 graphics.
